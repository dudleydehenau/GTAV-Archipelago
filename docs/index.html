    Archipelago randomizer · story mode

    Los Santos,shuffled.

    You start a new game in an empty city: no story, one character, no money. Michael, Franklin, Trevor, every mission, your guns and your cars are spread across the multiworld. Everything you do in Los Santos sends the other players their items.

    Story mode only, BattlEye off · Archipelago 0.6.4+

    How it plays

    Your map fills up as the others play

    A mission shows up on the map, in its character's colour, once you hold its item, the character who starts it and everyone who plays in it. Walk into the marker and it runs through the game's own replay system, cutscenes included.

    Every item arrives as a text from the player who sent it. One menu on F6 covers the rest: progress, missions, your goal step by step, hints, the garage and the map filters.

    53 traps

    Your friends can ruin your day

    Mixed into the pool with money, guns and cars. The notification only tells you who sent it. You choose which ones are allowed and how often they show up, and the ones that can't break a mission are allowed during missions too.

    About ten minutes

    Get on the road

    The setup brings ScriptHookVDotNet, LemonUI, the five mods and the apworld, and keeps them up to date. You only bring ScriptHookV, which can't be shared by anyone but its author.

    1. Run the setup

      Download GTAVArchipelagoSetup, run it and follow the four steps. It copies the apworld into Archipelago for you.

    2. Add ScriptHookV

      Download the zip for your game version from its page. The setup picks it up from your Downloads folder.

    3. Make your YAML

      Open the Options Creator, or start from a preset: Short, Ten Hours, One Mission, Free Roam, Everything, Stream Safe.

    4. Connect and drive

      Launch GTA V with BattlEye off, press F6, enter the room and your slot once, then start a New Game. It reconnects by itself after that.

    Goal option

    Pick your ending

    From the whole story to a single silly check.

    • finalePass one of the three final missions.
    • the_third_wayThe ending where all three of them make it.
    • mission_percentagePass a share of your missions, any of them.
    • one_missionOne mission, picked by you or by surprise.
    • one_collectibleOne random collectible. The client routes you to it.
    • one_checkOne random location. Short on purpose.
    • check_percentageA share of every location in your world.
    • free_roamA number of landmarks, stunts, skills and escapes.

    The final missions open only after a share of your other missions and the six heists, like in the story. No more finishing in twenty minutes because the right item came early.

    Under the hood

    Five mods, one menu

    Each one works on its own and has a public API, so other modders can build on them. The Archipelago client ties them together behind F6.

    • GameArchipelago client

      Connection, items, checks, hints, goals, the F6 menu, map filters and bug reports.

    • MissionLauncher missions

      Starts any story mission or Strangers and Freaks from its marker, with the right character and setup.

    • GameEnvironment world

      The empty starting world, the three characters, money, the garage, the new Ammu-Nation and the activities.

    • GameCollectibles free roam

      Every collectible and free-roam feat, with radar blips, routes and tracking.

    • GameTraps chaos

      The 53 traps, from Moon Gravity to Grand Theft Auto 1997.

    Story mode only

    Never take the mods into GTA Online. Click GTA Online mode in the setup and turn BattlEye back on before you go online.

    Cloud saves off

    If the game asks about cloud saves, click Not now. With them on, story mode can hang on the loading screen.

    Streaming

    One trap can show nudity. Turn on F6 › Settings › Stream safe, or start from the Stream Safe preset.

    Found a bug?

    F6 › Settings › Create a bug report makes a zip with your save and the logs. Send it on Discord or open a GitHub issue.
