Get started

The setup downloads ScriptHookVDotNet, LemonUI, the five mods and the APWorld, and keeps them up to date. You only bring ScriptHookV, which its author does not allow anyone to redistribute.

  1. Run the setup

    Download GTAVArchipelagoSetup from the latest release, run it and follow the four steps on screen.

  2. Add ScriptHookV

    Download the zip for your game version from its page. The setup finds it in your Downloads folder.

  3. Generate a game

    Drop gtav.apworld into Archipelago and start from a preset: Short, Ten Hours, One Mission, Everything or Stream Safe.

  4. Connect in game

    Launch GTA V with BattlEye off, press F6, open Connection, enter the room and your slot, then start a New Game.

Goal option

Choose how your run ends

From a full story playthrough to a one-check sprint. When your goal is done, the server can release your remaining items to the others.

finalePass one of the three final missions.
the_third_wayPass The Third Way, the ending where all three survive.
mission_percentagePass a share of all the missions in your world, any of them.
one_missionPass a single mission, chosen or random.
one_collectiblePick up one random collectible. The client routes you to it.
one_checkOne random location of your world. Silly on purpose.
check_percentageCheck a share of every location, whatever they are.
free_roamA number of free-roam checks: landmarks, stunts, skills, escapes, collectibles.
Under the hood

Five mods, one menu

Each mod works on its own and has a public API, so other modders can build on them. The Archipelago client ties them together behind F6.

ModWhat it does
GameArchipelagoThe Archipelago client: connection, items, checks, hints, goals, the F6 menu, the map filters and the bug report.
MissionLauncherStarts any story mission or Strangers and Freaks from a marker on the map, with the right character and setup.
GameEnvironmentThe empty starting world, the three characters, money, the garage, the new Ammu-Nation and every activity opened.
GameCollectiblesEvery collectible and free-roam feat, with radar blips, routes and completion tracking.
GameTrapsThe 53 traps, from Moon Gravity to Grand Theft Auto 1997.

Story mode only

Never take the mods into GTA Online. Before going online, click GTA Online mode in the setup and turn BattlEye back on.

Streaming?

One trap can show nudity. Turn on F6 › Settings › Stream safe, or start from the Stream Safe preset.

Found a bug?

F6 › Settings › Create a bug report makes a zip with your save and the logs. Send it on Discord or open a GitHub issue.
