ScriptHookVDotNet3.dll (3.7+) and LemonUI.SHVDN3.dll (2.x) go here. They are referenced only and are not copied
to the output.

MissionLauncher.dll, GameEnvironment.dll, GameTraps.dll and GameCollectibles.dll can go here too. If they are
missing, the project uses the Release or Debug build of the sibling folders (..\MissionLauncher\bin\Release\net48\...),
so building GTAV-Archipelago.sln builds everything in the right order.

Archipelago.MultiClient.Net and Newtonsoft.Json come from NuGet and are copied to the output.
